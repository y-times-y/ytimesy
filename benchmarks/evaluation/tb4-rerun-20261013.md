Committed before any replacement trial starts.

## What happened

The full run's coordinator VM (`vm-08ba0dca19444f33869401ccc78a4b00`) was created
with a 24-hour auto-delete. It deleted itself at about 2026-10-13 02:42 UTC with the
run's result files. A local backup taken 2026-10-12 ~14:09 UTC holds complete logs
(agent trace, verifier output, artifacts) for every scored trial of 63 tasks.

Scored trials whose logs were lost:

- `fp8-rmsnorm-gemm`: all 5 scored H100 trials (recorded score 3/5).
- `ctr-optimization`: 1 of 5 (`ctr-optimization__bEM3uE3`, recorded as a pass; the
  backup holds its action log up to 2026-10-12 13:50 UTC but not its end).

Never graded: `jax-speedrun-gpu` (two attempts cancelled, a third killed with the VM).

## Rule

1. Replacement trials: `fp8-rmsnorm-gemm` x5, `ctr-optimization` x1, `jax-speedrun-gpu` x5.
2. Same frozen harness (`039bee1`, label `v13-shell-039bee1`), same model and settings,
   same pinned task images, Harbor 0.24.0.
3. The replacement trials replace the lost ones whatever they score. No trial is
   rerun because of its result. The existing scoring rule still applies: a trial whose
   agent never started, whose grading environment failed to build, or whose host the
   provider killed is rerun, not scored.
4. The write-up reports the lost trials' recorded scores (fp8 3/5, the ctr-optimization
   pass) alongside the replacement results, and the providers used.
5. Providers: `fp8-rmsnorm-gemm` on Modal H100 (Daytona allows one GPU at a time);
   `jax-speedrun-gpu` on Daytona H100, one trial at a time; `ctr-optimization` on
   Daytona CPU. Every GPU is checked as an NVIDIA H100 before a scored trial.
6. Results are copied off the coordinator after every trial; the coordinator has no
   auto-delete.

### What failed on 2026-10-13

- `fp8-rmsnorm-gemm` x5 on Modal: the Modal account hit its billing-cycle spend limit at
  ~10:33 UTC; every agent and verifier build was terminated. No trial graded.
- `jax-speedrun-gpu` attempt 1 (`EG7gn9q`): the agent's `/app` was 63 GB; Harbor's Daytona
  directory transfer (600 s pack limit) failed and the grader ran on an empty `/app`.
- `jax-speedrun-gpu` attempts 1, 3 (`E4RERNc`) and 4 (`Ki2V6ST`), and two preflights: the
  agent was ended by an OpenAI response stream that closed mid-response with no terminal
  event and no error. Replaying a captured cut request four times completed normally each
  time: the cut is intermittent on the provider path, not caused by the request. The
  frozen harness does not replay a stream (`streamRetries: 0`), so one cut ends the agent.
- Attempt 2 (`677j7Ev`) was stopped by the operator before grading, ahead of the transfer fix.

### Changes (infrastructure only)

1. Providers: `fp8-rmsnorm-gemm` runs on Daytona H100 (Modal budget exhausted), one trial at
   a time after `jax-speedrun-gpu`. Both snapshots are checked as NVIDIA H100 before each task.
2. Workspace transfer (Harbor startup patch, `daytonapatch/sitecustomize.py`): system tar with
   gzip -1 and 4 h limits; tar's "file changed as we read it" tolerated. Workspaces over 4 GB
   go from the agent sandbox to R2 over pre-signed part URLs and into the grader over a signed
   URL. Same bytes, same directories. Verified: 20 GB with matching SHA-256 on both sides.
3. Transport guard (Node preload, `transport-guard.cjs`): each streamed OpenAI response is
   held until its terminal event, then delivered whole. If OpenAI closes a stream without a
   terminal event, the identical request is sent again (at most 3 times). The agent never
   receives a partial response, so no work it observed is replayed. Every call is logged
   with its OpenAI request id.

### Classification (`classify.py`, applied to every trial)

- Valid: the agent started, `/app` reached the grader, a reward was recorded, and no
  provider cut that the guard could not recover ended the agent. Ordinary outcomes (wrong
  answer, gave up, agent time limit) are always valid.
- Infra (rerun, not scored): anything else, including the attempts above (`EG7gn9q`,
  `677j7Ev`, `E4RERNc`, `Ki2V6ST`) and every Modal fp8 trial.
- A grader that times out records no reward, so the trial is rerun, as the original run's
  scorer (`pertask.py`) treated every trial without a reward. Attempts are capped at 12 for
  `jax-speedrun-gpu` and 10 for `fp8-rmsnorm-gemm`.
- The original 63-task run counted 2 trials that ended on an empty final model response;
  there is no transport evidence for them, so they stay as recorded and are disclosed.

### Budget
